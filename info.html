How It Started

I created the Daydream Oracle Deck for myself, but am constantly astounded by the amount of people who now use it daily and am so incredibly grateful.

When I first started using decks, Oracle, Tarot and otherwise, I'd often get discouraged at the complexity. I wanted something that was simple and direct, while also allowing for intuitive guidance or clarification.

The Origin

The Daydream Oracle was born in January of 2022 as I recovered from a breakup on my sisters couch. Jobless yet financially abundant for the first time in my adult life, I found myself with long hours of the day alone to create and knew that this was the project where my energy belonged.

After a long conversation with a friend (and a little bit of morning Maryjane) I channeled the entire list of cards. Sure, there were a few titles changed later on for specificity - but most of the deck came out of me that morning, in order and numerologically aligned. That was the first (huge) nudge from the Universe that I was on the right path.

By April of that year I had the real deck in my hands, and was lucky enough to sell all 100 within just a few years. I may or may not produce a second run - but that was a big motivator for creating this site, I wanted everyone to have access to the Daydream Oracle Deck's wisdom.

What Makes This Different

This site, as you will notice is not like many others out there. The Past, Present, Future spread, and upcoming spreads are 100% unique and trained on hours and hours of my transcribed readings, articles and the Daydream Oracle Book - these things were all created by me, not by a bot - directly from my brain.

Thank You

Creating this deck and holding space for others in readings is a small, but valuable part of my life. Thank you for being here and joining our journeys, even if for only a brief moment in time.

If this space brings you joy, guidance or value of any kind, consider sending me a little love via tip - I want to keep it free and available for as long as possible.

How to Use This Deck

Daydream Oracle is designed to be simple. You don't need experience with oracle or tarot cards to use it. There are two ways to connect with the deck: draw a single card for a direct message from the universe, or lay out a full Past, Present, Future spread for a deeper look at where you have been, where you are, and where you are headed. Each of the 60 cards carries a specific theme and message. Trust the card you pull - it is never random.

FAQ

What is an oracle card reading? +

An oracle card reading is an intuitive practice where you draw one or more cards from a deck as a way of accessing guidance, clarity, or perspective. Unlike tarot, oracle decks don't follow a fixed structure - each deck reflects the vision of its creator. Daydream Oracle was designed to give you a direct, honest message without requiring any prior knowledge of card reading.

How is oracle different from tarot? +

Tarot follows a fixed 78-card structure with established archetypes and suits. Oracle decks are freeform - the creator defines the cards, the themes, and the meaning. Daydream Oracle is a 60-card deck built around emotional states, actions, and energetic themes that speak directly to everyday life.

Is Daydream Oracle free? +

Yes - completely. This site and all of its readings are free to use. If it brings you value, a tip is always appreciated, but never expected.

Who created the Daydream Oracle deck? +

Daydream Oracle was created by Paige Woods in January 2022. The deck, the readings, the Past Present Future spread, and all written content were created by Paige - not generated by AI.

Who created this site? +

Can I do a reading right now? +

Yes. Head to the main deck, draw a single card, or lay out a full Past, Present, Future spread. No account needed, no sign up, no cost.
