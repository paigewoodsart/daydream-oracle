Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

The short version

Daydream Oracle does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Anything you save stays on your own device.

Who we are

Daydream Oracle is created and published by Paige Woods, a sole proprietor based in Washington State, USA. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Paige Woods, and "the app" means the Daydream Oracle app.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, health information, purchases, device identifiers, browsing history or usage data. The app has no sign-up or log-in.

What stays on your device

When you tap Save, the app stores the cards you drew, the date and the reading text in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Saved readings stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete any single reading from the Saved screen. Deleting the app removes all saved readings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for you to request from us, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Consent

Saving a reading is always your choice. You can withdraw that choice at any time by deleting the reading or the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not share data with any third party. If you turn on "Share with App Developers" in your device's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. Those reports are handled under Apple's own privacy policy, and we use them only to fix problems in the app.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because the app collects none, we hold nothing to access or delete. You can still contact us at any time.

Security

Saved readings are kept in the app's private storage and are protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and Face ID.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top. If the app ever begins to collect any data, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy details before it does.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form at daydreamoracle.com.
